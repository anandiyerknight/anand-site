To switch the machine on.

Work starts the day the advance clears and the access items below are in hand. Everything shared here is covered by the confidentiality terms in the final box. If you already have a sales team, the only addition is telling me where qualified leads should land and who owns them.

01 · Access & credentials

The accounts the machine runs on

  • LinkedIn account used for outreach (profile + company page)
  • Instagram account, and Meta Business Manager full admin
  • Google Analytics access, if a website is in scope
  • A company-domain email + Google account for outbound and tool logins
02 · Lead handoff & ownership

Where qualified leads go

  • The destination: your CRM, a shared sheet, or a team inbox (plus access)
  • The owner: the person or team who receives and closes
  • Your definition of a qualified lead (ICP fit + intent), so I automate to it
  • A follow-up SLA: how fast your team works a handed-off lead
03 · Context & reference

So it qualifies in your voice

  • Your ICP: industry, role, company size, geography

  • Existing clients + logo files for social proof in content
04 · Brand & creative

So content looks like you

  • Brand guidelines: colors, fonts, tone of voice
  • Logo files in vector and standard formats
  • Any prior content for reference (carousels, brochures, videos)
05 · Commercial

To begin and to run

  • Platform budgets: WhatsApp API, contact research at ₹1 per contact, Instagram on Scale, billed at actuals with receipts
06 · Confidentiality

How your data is treated

  • Everything shared is strictly confidential, used solely for this engagement
  • Governed by a signed NDA. Nothing is repurposed or retained beyond scope
